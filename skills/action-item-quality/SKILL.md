---
name: Action item quality
description: What separates a commitment somebody can be held to from a pleasantry.
applies_to: always
---

A next step is only worth recording if a person reading it next week could tell whether it happened.

Record a next step when the call contains all three of:

- **someone specific** who will do it — a named person or a clearly identified side, not "the team"
  when nobody said which team
- **a thing that will exist** afterwards — a document sent, a meeting booked, a signature, an
  access grant, an answer given. Something with a before and an after
- **a rough when** — a date, a day, "before the board meeting", "by end of week". "Soon" is not a
  when, but "after they get back from leave" is, because the trigger is observable

Do not record:

- enthusiasm mistaken for commitment — being keen is not a next step
- things the rep merely offered where the other side did not take them up
- restating a step already agreed earlier in the same call; record it once, at its clearest mention
- anything you would have to infer from account context rather than hear on the call

If a commitment is real but vague — the caller clearly agreed to something without saying what —
record it with exactly the specificity the call had. Do not sharpen it. An action item that is more
precise than the conversation misrepresents what the other side actually signed up to, and the line
you cite will not support the detail you added.

Prefer fewer, firmer items. Three commitments a rep can chase beat eight that include four maybes.
