Project Documentation · OpenGong Lite

Deal notes with receipts.

Upload a sales call. Get a transcript, a summary, objections, intent, next steps and a follow-up email — where every claim points at the exact line in the call that proves it. Claims that cannot be traced to a real line are deleted, not softened. This site explains what exists, how it works, and what is still unfinished.

In one minute

The problem. Every meeting-notes tool writes you a summary. None of them let you check it. A confident paragraph about "budget concerns" is worthless if nobody can find where the prospect said it — and actively harmful if they never did.

What we built. The same notes, plus a hard rule: no proof, no claim. Every objection, next step and intent label carries the transcript line it came from. A blocking gate checks each citation before you see anything. Click a claim in the UI and the audio jumps to the second it came from.

The unusual part. The app shows you what it threw away — the claims the model produced that failed verification, with reasons. Notes you cannot audit are a confident guess.
